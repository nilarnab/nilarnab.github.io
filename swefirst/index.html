Neurocomputing, Volume 588, Article 1276232024

A visual question and answering system with support for compound emotions using facial landmark identification with MediaPipe and CNN classifier

Lavika Goel, Nilarnab Debnath, and Sanskar Mundaniya.

The paper extends visual question answering to questions about facial emotion. The system combines attention-based image captioning with RetinaFace and DeepFace for face and basic-emotion detection, then uses MediaPipe facial landmarks and a CNN to recognize compound emotions before answering questions with BERT. Its modular design allows the captioning and question-answering components to be trained independently.

Qwen2.5 · GSM8K · SFT · GRPOCurrent research

Prompt Invariant Training for robust mathematical reasoning

Developed a post-training framework that makes LLM mathematical reasoning less sensitive to irrelevant or adversarial changes in a prompt. Used GPT-OSS to incrementally inject noise into GSM8K questions, then trained Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct with original reasoning traces and adversarial examples using supervised fine-tuning and GRPO.

Improved adversarial accuracy from 21.6% at baseline to 31.3% with SFT and 41.6% with GRPO. GRPO also increased clean reasoning accuracy from 65.7% to 80.2%, outperforming standard SFT at 58.8%.
